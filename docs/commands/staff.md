# Staff commands

Access depends on Discord permissions, role hierarchy, Red checks, and server policy.
Review direct help before destructive or bulk actions.

## Moderation

- Warnings: `[p]warn`, `[p]unwarn`, `[p]warnings`, `[p]reasonlist`, and `[p]actionlist`
- Mutes: `[p]mute`, `[p]unmute`, `[p]mutechannel`, and `[p]unmutechannel`
- Removal: `[p]kick`, `[p]ban`, `[p]softban`, `[p]tempban`, `[p]unban`, and `[p]massban`
- Cases: `[p]case`, `[p]casesfor`, `[p]listcases`, and `[p]reason`
- Utilities: `[p]userinfo`, `[p]names`, `[p]rename`, `[p]cleanup`, and `[p]report`

## Roles and community features

- `[p]addrole`, `[p]removerole`, `[p]selfrole`, and `[p]selfroleset`
- `[p]roletools` and `[p]gameroleset`
- `[p]giveaway`, `[p]welcomeset`, `[p]streamalert`, `[p]streamset`, and `[p]rss`

## Game and media configuration

- `[p]arkset`, `[p]dayzset`, `[p]gmodset`, and `[p]icarusset`
- `[p]cocset`
- `[p]rocketleagueset` (alias `[p]rlset`) and `[p]rlcsset`
- `[p]movieset`, `[p]navidromeset`, and `[p]memeset`
- `[p]audioset`, `[p]playlist`, and `[p]eq`

## Server policy

- `[p]filter` and `[p]filterset`
- `[p]warningset`, `[p]warnaction`, and `[p]warnreason`
- `[p]muteset`, `[p]modset`, and `[p]modlogset`
- `[p]command`, `[p]ignore`, `[p]unignore`, and `[p]alias`

Delegated feature access does not grant bot-host access. Loading cogs, changing global
credentials, restarting the process, and repository management remain maintainer tasks.
