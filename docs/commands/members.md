# Member commands

## Bot and community

- `[p]help`, `[p]info`, `[p]invite`, `[p]uptime`, `[p]contact`, and `[p]mydata`
- `[p]selfroles`, `[p]gameroles`, and `[p]mywarnings`
- Giveaways and role menus may use buttons or reactions rather than typed commands.

## Games and server lookups

- `[p]ark`, `[p]dayz`, `[p]gmod`, and `[p]icarus`
- `[p]coc` for Clash of Clans
- `[p]rocketleague` (alias `[p]rl`) and `[p]rlcs`
- `[p]rlstats` and `[p]rlsports` when the legacy stats integration is enabled
- `[p]runescape` (alias `[p]rs`)

## Music, media, and discovery

- `[p]play`, `[p]queue`, `[p]now`, `[p]skip`, `[p]pause`, `[p]stop`, and `[p]volume`
- `[p]meme`, `[p]memeify`, and `[p]movies`
- `[p]navidrome`
- `[p]picarto`, `[p]twitchstream`, and `[p]youtubestream`
- `[p]wolfram`, `[p]wolframimage`, and `[p]wolframsolve`

## Profiles, economy, and utilities

- `[p]bank`, `[p]leaderboard`, `[p]payday`, and `[p]slot`
- `[p]pf`, `[p]myprofile`, `[p]lvltop`, `[p]weekly`, and `[p]stars`
- `[p]choose`, `[p]flip`, `[p]roll`, `[p]rps`, `[p]serverinfo`, and `[p]trivia`

## Assistant

Common commands include `[p]chathelp`, `[p]chat`, `[p]convostats`,
`[p]convocompact`, `[p]convoclear`, `[p]convoshow`, and `[p]query`. Availability and
cost controls vary by server.

Never send passwords, tokens, recovery codes, private keys, or other secrets to any
bot command.
